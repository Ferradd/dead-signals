Every strategy we tested was already dead.

The folklore says: catch a fresh Solana memecoin early, ride it, exit before the music stops. We built the dataset, ran twenty variants of that playbook, and audited the results until nothing was left standing.

§03

Four audits, four deaths

Q1Independent recount

Fresh code re-derived every result from raw files — no shared functions, no shared assumptions. Sampled exit prices matched the tape 10/10. The results didn't survive: n=10 is an anecdote, and a single trade carried 42% of one group's entire EV.DET/Q1_RECOUNT.md

H18: +352.4 → +200.8 SOL (−43%)
Q2Point-in-time audit

For every feature: was this knowable at decision time? The leaderboards table stores fetch-time state — snapshot_date is NULL on every row. "Was this a leaderboard wallet when it bought?" silently became "is it one now?" The edge was the future leaking backward.DET/GRAVEYARD.md:5

H11: +73.2 → +0.19 SOL (−99.7%)
Q3Selection audit

Surviving signals fired almost exclusively in the top volume quintile — where the base winner rate is already 23.7% vs 6.0% overall. Volume-matched controls collapsed the lift to ~1.24×. The signal was a volume proxy. And two dev wallets accounted for ~12,500 SOL of aggregate P&L — a "population pattern" made of two actors.DET/Q3_SELECTION.md

H17: 85% of P&L from one dev wallet
Q4Execution audit

Exits assumed zero price impact — infinite liquidity, the oldest lie in backtesting. Replaced with constant-product math plus a +2% sandwich tax. The audit then caught a bare except swallowing a NameError on every time-stop exit: 539 of 1,618 simulated trades had silently vanished — selectively deleting the ugly exits that drag EV down.DET/Q4_EXECUTION.md

§04

The tape is hostile anyway

Even a real signal would face this microstructure: over 50% of launches are sniped in block 0 (median first buy: 0 seconds). ~17% of all transactions are wash trades, and wash trading raises graduation odds up to 10× — so the label "graduated" is itself partly manufactured, and every feature trained on it inherits the manufacturing.DET/DETECTIVE_BIBLE.md

§05

The exception

One artifact survived — modestly

A 60-second regime-gated scorer: +0.087 SOL/trade out-of-sample, n=1,192, 53.4% win rate. Regime-dependent, thin-margined, not a launch signal. Reported because "everything died" is also a claim that needs an exception check.

§06

Verdict

Verdict
20 / 20 — NO-GO

What survived isn't a strategy. It's the pipeline: independent recount, point-in-time validation, volume-matched controls, execution-aware simulation. If a backtest lacks a PIT check and an independent recount, it isn't a backtest — it's a bedtime story. Write the audits first next time.
